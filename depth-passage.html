Experimental depth-first playable level
Main game
Starting ledgeCharacter 100%Camera 1.00×

How to play: Tap a glowing neighboring platform or use Previous / Next to jump between ledges. The path zigzags left, right, upward and downward while progressing deeper into the cave. The character changes world scale during the approach; the camera waits until landing and then zooms with an adjustable slow start. Reverse direction to return toward the foreground. This is an isolated experiment; your existing cave maps are not accessed.
